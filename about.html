Since 2012

A family kitchen that grew up

What started as a ten-table dining room is now one of the city's most loved restaurants. We kept the values that built us: honest ingredients, patient cooking and genuine hospitality.

Our menu changes with the seasons, guided by what local farmers, fishermen and bakers bring through our door each morning.
